About Revive Realty and Property Group

One trusted partner for every real estate decision

Revive Realty and Property Group is a boutique Florida real estate and property management firm based in Pinellas Park, serving owners, buyers, sellers, and investors across Tampa Bay. Our promise is simple: honest guidance, hands-on service, and a licensed professional who is genuinely invested in your outcome.

Talk with us Call (727) 755-1662

Our story

Boutique by choice, thorough by habit

Real estate is rarely a single decision — it is a season of them. Most people find themselves working with one company to buy, another to sell, and yet another to manage a rental, repeating their story every time. Revive Realty and Property Group was founded to fix that: to give Tampa Bay one trusted, licensed partner for the whole journey.

We are a boutique firm on purpose. That means you deal directly with a real, local professional — not a call center and not a rotating cast of names. Whether you are protecting an investment property, buying your first home, listing to sell for top dollar, or building a portfolio, you get the same steady guidance and straight answers from start to finish, and long after closing.

Everything we do comes back to our name. We help owners revive the value of a property, help buyers and sellers move forward with confidence, and help investors turn a good decision into a lasting one — managed, maintained, and maximized.

Our mission

To be Tampa Bay’s most trusted home for real estate — protecting every client’s investment and maximizing every opportunity.

We measure success by the trust we earn: owners who sleep easier, buyers and sellers who feel guided instead of sold to, and investors who come back because the numbers — and the relationship — hold up over time.

What we stand for

The values behind every decision we make

These are not slogans on a wall. They are the standards we hold ourselves to on every property, every deal, and every conversation.

Integrity first

We give you the honest answer, even when it is not the easy one. No pressure, no spin — just advice we would stand behind if the property were our own.

Local expertise

Pinellas Park is home, and Tampa Bay is our market. We know these neighborhoods, price points, and rental dynamics because we work here every single day.

Transparent communication

Steady updates, clear reporting, and plain language. You always know exactly where things stand — with your property, your deal, and your money.

Protecting your investment

Every recommendation is made to preserve value and reduce risk — careful screening, proactive maintenance, and disciplined decisions that protect what you have built.

In the community
