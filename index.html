Why Choose Revive

Local, licensed, and genuinely invested in your outcome

You are not a ticket in a queue. When you work with Revive Realty and Property Group, you get a responsive, Florida-based professional who treats your property and your goals like they matter — because they do.

Thorough tenant screening

Credit, background, income, and rental-history checks help place reliable, qualified residents in your property.

Professional photography

Every listing is presented with professional-grade photography that helps homes lease and sell faster.

Online owner portal

Track statements, payments, and maintenance anytime through a secure online owner portal — full transparency, 24/7.

Transparent communication

Straight answers, steady updates, and no jargon. You always know exactly where things stand.

Quarterly property inspections

Through our partner CheckMyRental, every managed home gets photo-documented quarterly inspections — so tenant damage is caught early and charged to them, not left for you at lease-end.
